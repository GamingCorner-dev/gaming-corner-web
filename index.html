About Us

Gaming Corner was founded on July 27, 2025, specifically on a Discord server. The formation of Gaming Corner started from a gathering of gamer friends who loved playing games together, chatting, having fun, and discussing, which eventually led to the creation of the Gaming Corner Community.

Gaming Corner is your ultimate hangout spot to play any game with friends! Chill, make new friends, join fun events, meet awesome gamers, and win real MONEY in giveaways. Join us now!
